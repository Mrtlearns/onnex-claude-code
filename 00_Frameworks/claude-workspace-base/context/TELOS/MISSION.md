# Mission

> Core purposes — the "why" behind everything else. Stable, rarely changes.

---

## M0: Primary Mission

Leverage technology and AI to help SME businesses operate with the efficiency and intelligence of enterprise companies — without the enterprise cost or complexity.

**Why it matters:** SMEs represent the majority of businesses but lack access to the tools and systems that make large companies effective.
**How you pursue it:** Build and deliver AI-assisted Operating Systems that replace fragmented manual processes with unified, intelligent systems.
**Time horizon:** Ongoing

---

## M1: Organizational Mission

Onnex delivers AI-assisted Operating Systems to SME clients. Every engagement follows ATOM (Audit → Transform → Optimize → Maintain) and meets QDOAA gates (Quality, Delivery, Operations, Analytics, Adoption).
