# Mission

## M0: Primary Mission

Leverage technology and AI to help SME businesses operate with the efficiency and intelligence of enterprise companies — without the enterprise cost or complexity. SMEs represent the majority of businesses but lack access to the tools and systems that make large companies effective. Build and deliver AI-assisted Operating Systems that replace fragmented manual processes with unified, intelligent systems.

## M1: Organizational Mission

Onnex delivers AI-assisted Operating Systems to SME clients. Every engagement follows ATOM (Audit → Transform → Optimize → Maintain) and meets QDOAA gates (Quality, Delivery, Operations, Analytics, Adoption).

## M2: Personal Mission

To deliver an AI maturity assessment platform that drives Onnex client acquisition and paid product revenue
