# Beliefs

## B1: SMEs deserve enterprise-grade tools
Most AI tooling is built for enterprise. SMEs have the same operational complexity but a fraction of the resources. The opportunity is massive.

## B2: Self-hosted > SaaS for SME clients
SaaS creates dependency and recurring cost. Self-hosted systems give clients ownership and reduce churn risk.

## B3: Speed of response is the primary value driver in service businesses
Whoever responds first wins the client. This applies to PI law, NDT, MSPs, and most service verticals.

## B4: AI should be the operating layer, not a feature
Adding AI as a feature to existing broken processes doesn't work. The process must be rebuilt around AI capabilities.

## B5: Visibility is the prerequisite to AI adoption
Most SMEs don't resist AI — they don't know where to start. An assessment gives them a map. Without a clear picture of where they stand, no tool, consultant, or strategy lands. Measurement creates the entry point for transformation.
