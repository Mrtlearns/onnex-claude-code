# Strategies

## S1: Speed-to-Lead as Entry Hook
Use response time automation as the primary value demo for any client vertical. Fast wins build trust before deep system integration.

## S2: Vertical-First Specialization
Build one complete AI-OS per vertical before expanding. Depth beats breadth at the SME level.

## S3: Self-Hosted Infrastructure
Deploy on client infrastructure or Onnex-managed VPS — never SaaS lock-in. Reduces ongoing costs and builds client trust.

## S4: Assessment-as-Entry-Hook for Onnex Pipeline
Use the AI Maturity Compass as the primary client acquisition instrument. Free tier lowers barrier to entry and qualifies SMEs. Paid tier (deeper reports, benchmarks, white-label) generates direct revenue. The internal report (costing, training syllabus, engagement scope) is the Trojan horse — it frames the next Onnex consulting engagement naturally. Multi-channel: Onnex uses it internally for sales calls, SMEs self-serve online, consulting firms license it as a white-label tool.
