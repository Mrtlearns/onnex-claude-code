## Project

ai-maturity-compass — AI maturity assessment tool — Help SMEs measure and improve their AI maturity across 8 dimensions

## Current Focus Period

7 days (MVP by 2026-04-16)

## Strategic Priorities

1. **Ship a real, demo-able backend in 7 days** — Auth + DB + AI edge functions on poc-backend. The frontend is done. Every day is backend execution.

2. **Make the AI output genuinely useful, not generic** — The differentiation lives in report quality. Executive summaries and dimension narratives must be specific to the org's actual scores, not templated filler. This is what separates the product from a survey tool.

3. **Produce a marketing asset during the sprint** — A shareable report from a live run (real org, real data) is the primary sales instrument. Build it in parallel with the product. It enables immediate client conversations on day 8.

## What Success Looks Like

- End-to-end assessment flow works: employee registers → completes questionnaire → admin triggers evaluation → report generated from real DB data
- Shareable report link works publicly with no internal data exposed
- AI executive summary and narratives are specific, credible, and useful to a non-technical business owner
- One polished sample report or demo video exists and is ready to share in sales conversations by 2026-04-16

## Key Open Questions

- **Pricing:** What's the paid tier boundary — what does free give vs. what requires payment? (full report, AI features, white-label, multiple cycles?)
- **Distribution channel:** How does the first SME find this — outbound from Onnex, LinkedIn, referral, or do you have a specific prospect in mind already?
