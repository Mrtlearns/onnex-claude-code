# Mission

## M0: Primary Mission

Leverage technology and AI to help SME businesses operate with the efficiency and intelligence of enterprise companies — without the enterprise cost or complexity.

## M1: Organizational Mission

Onnex delivers AI-assisted Operating Systems to SME clients. Every engagement follows ATOM (Audit → Transform → Optimize → Maintain) and meets QDOAA gates (Quality, Delivery, Operations, Analytics, Adoption).

## M2: Project Mission

Deliver a purpose-built CMMC Level 2 compliance management platform for MSPs — replacing expensive, generic GRC tools (Drata, Vanta, RegScale) with an MSP-native, Claude-powered system that guides ~80,000 U.S. defense contractors through NIST SP 800-171 certification at a fraction of the cost.
