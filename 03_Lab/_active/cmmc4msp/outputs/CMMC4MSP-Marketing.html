The problem: 80,000+ U.S. defense contractors must achieve CMMC Level 2 certification by 2026 or lose DoD contract eligibility. Most MSPs today manage this in Excel spreadsheets — manually tracking 110 controls, chasing evidence uploads, and writing SSP documents by hand. That takes 8–10 weeks per client and leaves your team doing the same work over and over.
The Solution
Built for MSPs. Not generic GRC.

Unlike Drata, Vanta, or RegScale — which are generic compliance tools bolted onto CMMC — this platform was designed ground-up for CMMC Level 2. It knows every control, every assessment objective, every acceptable proof type, and every scoring rule.

🏢

MSP-Native Multi-Tenancy

One operator dashboard manages unlimited client organizations. Full data isolation — client A cannot see client B's data, ever. Role hierarchy enforced at the database layer.

🤖

Claude LLM Assessment

Every uploaded artifact is assessed by Claude — not keyword matching. The LLM reads the document and determines whether it satisfies the specific control objectives, just like a C3PAO would.

⚡

Evidence Automation

Integrations with Entra ID, Okta, Defender, CrowdStrike, M365, and Splunk pull evidence nightly. Harvester scripts auto-collect 25+ controls from endpoints in one run.

Platform Capabilities
21 features. Zero spreadsheets.

Every feature is in production, tested end-to-end, and deployed on the live platform at app.cmmc4msp.on-nex.us.

Evidence & Assessment

1

AI Artifact Assessment

Claude reads every uploaded document and returns a structured pass/partial/fail verdict with rationale and gap list — grounded in the control's exact assessment objectives.

2

Evidence Automation Hub

Single page surfacing all automation tools: integrations, bulk request, AI interview-to-artifact, harvester scripts, and ZIP upload.

3

6 Direct Integrations

Entra ID · Okta · Microsoft Defender · CrowdStrike · M365 Secure Score · Splunk. Nightly sync — evidence pulled, assessed, and applied automatically.

4

Harvester Scripts

Windows PS1 + Linux SH. Run on client endpoints — 12 collectors fire in parallel, auto-ZIP, auto-upload, auto-assess. ~25 controls in 30 minutes.

5

Bulk Evidence Request

Select any controls, choose assignee, add due date and instructions — one consolidated email dispatched listing every assigned control.

6

Evidence Freshness + Drift

Nightly monitoring detects stale evidence (by control family threshold) and material document changes since last assessment. Auto-flags for re-upload.

AI Assistance

7

Compliance Copilot (per control)

RAG-powered Claude chatbot on every control detail page, grounded in: control requirements, uploaded artifacts, NIST 800-171A assessment guide. Answers "what do I upload?" with precision.

8

Save Interview as Evidence

Copilot conversations can be saved as artifacts. The transcript is assessed by Claude — useful for controls satisfied by organizational practice rather than documents.

9

Task Queue Inline Copilot

AI guidance embedded inside each task card — contributors get "what to upload" answers without leaving their work list.

10

AI Policy Draft Generation

Claude generates org-specific policy documents (Access Control Policy, IR Plan, etc.) from hardware/software inventory. Output: reviewed DOCX, assessed automatically.

11

SSP Narrative Interview

15-question guided interview generates all 5 SSP narrative sections. ~45 minutes. Without it, SPRS is forced to −203 (DoD rule). Eliminates 8–20 hours of consultant writing.

12

Program AI Sweep

MSP-only. One click analyzes every open control — returns ranked action plan: which controls to attack first, what evidence to collect, and why. Bulk status apply.

Scoring, Reports & Operations

13

Live SPRS Scoring

Real-time score from −203 to 110. SSP gate rule enforced (missing SSP → −203 regardless). Recalculates automatically after every assessment.

14

C3PAO Audit Package

One click: SSP PDF + POA&M PDF + all artifacts + SHA-256 manifest + control-to-evidence map. Ready to hand to the assessor in under 2 minutes.

15

Platform Observability

Structured logging, correlation IDs across all services, AI error triage (nightly email to MSP admin with root-cause analysis), 6-component health endpoint.
