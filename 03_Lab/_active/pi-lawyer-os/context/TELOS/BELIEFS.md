# Beliefs

## B1: Speed-to-Lead is the primary lever in PI law
The firm that responds first wins the client in the majority of cases. Everything else — case management, document AI, billing — is secondary to this single variable.

## B2: PI firms do not need another CRM
They need a system that fills their CRM with signed clients automatically. Competing on features with Litify is a losing game. Competing on case acquisition outcomes is a winning game.

## B3: TCPA compliance is a competitive moat, not a constraint
Firms that do AI outreach compliantly will outperform firms doing it sloppily. Getting this right early creates defensible trust with clients.

## B4: Shared infrastructure scales the business
Every hour spent building PI-specific infrastructure that could have been shared with NDT or MSP is wasted. The platform approach is the right approach even if it takes longer upfront.

## B5: Local market first, national second
Trying to sell nationwide before proving the model locally is a distraction. Close 3 Las Vegas PI firms. Build the case study. Then expand.
