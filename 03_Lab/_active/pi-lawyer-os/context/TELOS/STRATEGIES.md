# Strategies

## S1: Speed-to-Lead as Primary Hook and Demo
Lead every prospect conversation with the case acquisition angle. The demo shows: lead comes in → AI responds in under 60 seconds → firm gets notified. This is visceral and immediately valuable to any PI attorney.

## S2: Build on Shared AI-OS Infrastructure
PI Growth OS is a vertical module on the Onnex AI-OS platform — not a standalone app. PostgreSQL, Temporal, Hasura, n8n, Docker Compose all reused. Reduces per-client build time and maintenance cost.

## S3: Las Vegas First
Target Las Vegas PI firms before expanding geographically. Local presence enables in-person demos and faster trust-building. Las Vegas PI market is large (high accident volume) and less saturated with tech-forward firms.

## S4: ShareCRM Partnership for Warm Intros
Use the ShareCRM NDT relationship as a bridge into PI referral networks. Legal and NDT communities overlap in personal injury (workplace accidents, construction injuries). Warm intros beat cold outreach.
