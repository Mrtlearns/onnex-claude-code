CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: Aerospace NDT

GOVERNING STANDARDS:
  NAS 410 / EN 4179:     NDT personnel qualification (Level I/II/III)
  Nadcap AC7114:         RT process audit checklist (required for Nadcap accreditation)
  AMS 2175:              Casting inspection — classification and acceptance
  AMS 2635:              Radiographic inspection of metallic aerospace components
  Customer prime specs:  Boeing BAC series, Airbus AIMS, Lockheed, GE Aviation, Pratt & Whitney

TECHNIQUES (in priority order for aerospace):
  Conventional film RT:  MIL-STD-453 heritage; still required by many prime specs
  Digital Radiography (DR): ASTM E2597 — real-time, requires calibration artifacts
  Computed Radiography (CR): ASTM E2007 — phosphor plate; field-portable
  Computed Tomography (CT): ASTM E1695 — 3D volumetric; required for complex assemblies and FOD

CRITICAL FOR AEROSPACE:
  - FOD detection (foreign object debris embedded in assemblies — fastener holes, cavities)
  - Braze joint coverage verification (braze voids > acceptance limit are rejectable)
  - Composite delamination/disbond — X-ray limited; flag for C-scan ultrasonic instead
  - Tight tolerance defect sizing for fracture mechanics life calculations
  - Part-specific written procedures required — generic procedures are non-conforming
  - All RT must be performed to customer-approved written procedures

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: FOD locations, braze joint areas, fastener hole bores (stress concentration + assembly risk)
  HIGH:     Primary load-bearing welds/joints, spar-to-rib attachments, fitting-to-structure interfaces
  MEDIUM:   Secondary structural attachments, non-primary load path welds
  LOW:      Non-structural brackets, fairings, non-load-path attachments

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - Airfoil (blade/vane): LatheGeometry with airfoil profile points, or compound elliptical primitives
  - Skin panel: BoxGeometry(length, width, thickness) — very thin, large area
  - Spar (I or C section): ExtrudeGeometry with I/C cross-section shape
  - Rib: BoxGeometry or ExtrudeGeometry with lightening holes noted in features
  - Engine disk: CylinderGeometry(OD/2, ID/2, thickness, 64) — annular disk with bore
  - Engine ring: TorusGeometry or CylinderGeometry for ring geometry
  - Fitting/bracket: compound BoxGeometry + CylinderGeometry primitives
  - CT priority: any geometry where internal voids or complex intersections cannot be imaged in projection

PROCESS CONTROLS:
  - IQI: wire type per ASTM E747 (2% sensitivity or better required for aerospace)
  - Geometric unsharpness: Ug ≤ 0.020" for thin section (<0.25"); Ug ≤ 1/16" for thicker
  - All film must be processed to density 2.0–4.0 (D&B range per prime spec)
