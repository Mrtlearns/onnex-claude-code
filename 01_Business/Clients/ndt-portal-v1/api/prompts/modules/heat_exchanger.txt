CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: Heat Exchanger Radiographic Inspection

GOVERNING STANDARDS:
  ASME BPVC Section VIII Div. 1: Shell, channel, and head fabrication
  TEMA (Tubular Exchanger Manufacturers Association): RCB standards for shell-and-tube
  ASME Section V Article 2: RT examination technique
  HEI Standards: Heat Exchange Institute (for certain power plant exchangers)

KEY INSPECTION AREAS:
  1. Tube-to-tubesheet welds: critical seal welds, potential leak paths
  2. Shell longitudinal seams (Category A): full RT if UW-11(a) applies
  3. Shell girth seams (Category B): RT per code/customer requirement
  4. Channel-to-tubesheet joints (Category C): flange/channel attachment
  5. Nozzle attachment welds (Category D): inlet/outlet nozzles to shell and channel
  6. Pass partition welds: internal baffle/partition plate attachment welds
  7. Impingement plate attachments: local high-velocity erosion zone

TUBE-TO-TUBESHEET WELD LIMITATIONS:
  Standard film RT is generally not effective for individual tube-to-tubesheet welds due to
  geometric constraints (tube spacing, backscatter). Flag for alternative methods:
  - Eddy current (ET): standard method for in-service tube inspection
  - Borescope + PT: for accessible tube-end welds
  - Miniature crawler RT: specialized technique, limited availability
  Document this limitation explicitly in findings.

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: Tube-to-tubesheet weld zones (even if RT-limited — flag alternative method), nozzle attachment welds (Category D)
  HIGH:     Shell longitudinal seams (Category A), channel head seams
  MEDIUM:   Shell girth seams (Category B), pass partition welds
  LOW:      Support saddle/lug attachments, nameplate bracket welds

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - Shell: CylinderGeometry(shellOD/2, shellOD/2, shellLength, 64)
  - Tubesheet: CylinderGeometry(tsOD/2, tsOD/2, tsThickness, 64) — solid disk
  - Channel head: SphereGeometry (hemispherical) or CylinderGeometry (flat/cylindrical)
  - Floating head: SphereGeometry or elliptical head LatheGeometry profile
  - Nozzle inlet/outlet: CylinderGeometry on shell surface — specify OD and projection length
  - Saddle supports: two BoxGeometry primitives at support locations

TECHNIQUE NOTES:
  - Shell and head RT: same as pressure vessel — SWSI for girth seams
  - Nozzle RT: geometric setup critical — source in nozzle for elliptical projection, or set/double-wall
  - All shell seams to be examined per UW-11 requirements matching code stamp (ASME U or UM)
