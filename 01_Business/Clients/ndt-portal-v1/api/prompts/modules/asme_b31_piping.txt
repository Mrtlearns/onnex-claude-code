CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: ASME B31.3 Process Piping (also applicable to B31.1 Power Piping)

EXAMINATION EXTENT per Table 341.3.2:
  Normal service:       5% random RT of butt welds
  Severe cyclic:        100% RT of all butt welds
  Category M (lethal):  100% RT of all butt welds
  High pressure:        per Chapter IX requirements

WELD TYPES:
  Butt welds (girth and longitudinal) — primary RT candidates
  Branch connection welds (set-in, set-on, integrally reinforced)
  Socket welds — generally NOT RT; note limitation in findings
  Fillet welds — generally NOT RT; note limitation in findings

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: Table 341.3.2 + ASME Section V Article 2
In-service inspection per B31.3 Chapter VII if applicable

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: Branch connection welds (set-in/full-pen), girth welds at elbows and reducers (highest stress concentration)
  HIGH:     Full-penetration girth butt welds in straight runs, longitudinal seams if applicable
  MEDIUM:   Girth welds in low-stress/low-pressure sections (5% random selection candidates)
  LOW:      Socket weld areas (RT not applicable — note for UT/PT alternative)

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - Straight pipe: CylinderGeometry(OD/2, OD/2, length, 64)
  - Elbow (90°): TorusGeometry(bendRadius, OD/2, 16, 64, PI/2); position at bend centerline
  - Elbow (45°): TorusGeometry(bendRadius, OD/2, 16, 64, PI/4)
  - Reducer (concentric): CylinderGeometry(largeOD/2, smallOD/2, length, 64)
  - Reducer (eccentric): CylinderGeometry with offset + rotate; or LatheGeometry profile
  - Tee: compound — main CylinderGeometry + branch CylinderGeometry intersecting at 90°
  - Flange: CylinderGeometry(flangeOD/2, flangeOD/2, flangeThk, 64) — use as cap on pipe end
  - Cap/blind: SphereGeometry(OD/2, 32, 32, 0, 2*PI, 0, PI/2) or BoxGeometry for flat cap

TECHNIQUE NOTES:
  - Girth welds: SWSI preferred for pipe OD >= 3.5"; DWDI for smaller bore
  - Panoramic: use for thin-wall pipe where source can be placed inside
  - Digital radiography (DR): preferred for production weld inspection (faster, no film processing)
  - Crawlers: required for large diameter pipe where direct source placement is impractical
