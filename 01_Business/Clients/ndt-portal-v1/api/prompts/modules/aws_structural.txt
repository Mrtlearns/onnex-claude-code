CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Code — Steel
Also applicable to AWS D1.6 (Stainless Steel) where noted.

WELD CLASSIFICATIONS:
  CJP (Complete Joint Penetration): Full RT per Clause 6 where required
  PJP (Partial Joint Penetration): Generally NOT RT inspected — note alternative UT
  Fillet welds: NOT RT inspected — specify MT or PT in findings

RT REQUIREMENTS per Clause 6.12:
  Statically loaded:    RT per engineer's discretion or contract requirement
  Cyclically loaded (fatigue): RT per Table 6.1 stress category
  Fracture critical:    100% RT of all CJP welds

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: Clause 6.12.2 + Table 6.2
DISCONTINUITY CATEGORIES: elongated, rounded, cluster (per Table 6.2 size/location limits)

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: CJP welds at moment connections (beam-to-column), fracture-critical members, weld repairs
  HIGH:     CJP welds in cyclically loaded members, column splices, truss chord connections
  MEDIUM:   CJP welds in statically loaded members (engineer-discretion RT), gusset plate connections
  LOW:      PJP welds (RT not applicable — UT alternative), fillet welds (PT/MT alternative)

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - W-shape / I-beam: use three BoxGeometry primitives (two flanges + one web); or ExtrudeGeometry with I-shape
  - Channel: two BoxGeometry primitives (flange + web); or ExtrudeGeometry with C-shape
  - Hollow structural section (HSS): CylinderGeometry for round, BoxGeometry for square/rect
  - Plate: BoxGeometry(width, thickness, height)
  - Gusset: BoxGeometry(width, thickness, height) — triangular shape approximated
  - Stiffener: BoxGeometry(width, thickness, height) — thin plate on web
  - Base plate: BoxGeometry — use as foundation element
  - Beam-to-column connections: compound — beam primitives intersecting column primitive

CONNECTION ZONE PRIORITY:
  Beam-to-column moment connections are highest priority for RT
  Identify all CJP welds at connection zones as HIGH minimum; CRITICAL if fracture-critical
