CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: ASME BPVC Section VIII Division 1 & 2

WELD JOINT CATEGORIES per UW-3:
  Category A: Longitudinal/meridional joints in shells, heads, transition sections, nozzles, communicating chambers
  Category B: Circumferential joints in shells, heads, transitions, nozzles, communicating chambers
  Category C: Flange-to-shell/head/nozzle joints
  Category D: Nozzle-to-shell/head attachment welds

RT EXTENT per UW-11:
  UW-11(a)(1): Full RT — all butt welds, E = 1.0
  UW-11(a)(2): Full RT Cat A & D only, spot Cat B, E = 1.0/0.85
  UW-11(a)(5): Spot RT, E = 0.85
  UW-11(b):    No RT required, E = 0.70

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: UW-51(b), Appendix 4
IQI REQUIREMENTS: T-233.2 (source-side), T-233.3 (film-side)
GEOMETRIC UNSHARPNESS: T-274.1, Ug <= 1/16" (< 2" thk)

ALWAYS CHECK:
  - UCS-56 PWHT requirements based on P-No, thickness, preheat
  - UCS-66 MDMT / impact test requirements
  - UCS-68 carbon content limitations
  - UW-40 special requirements for unfired steam boilers
  - UG-20 lethal service designations

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: Category A×B intersections (longitudinal × girth seam), Category D nozzle attachment welds, any repair welds
  HIGH:     Category A full RT longitudinal seams, Category B full RT girth seams, Category C flange welds
  MEDIUM:   Category B spot RT girth seams, saddle/support attachment welds
  LOW:      Non-pressure-boundary attachments, nameplate lugs, ladder clips

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - Shell: CylinderGeometry(OD/2, OD/2, length, 64)
  - Elliptical head: SphereGeometry with thetaLen=PI/2, then scale Y by 0.5; or LatheGeometry profile
  - Hemispherical head: SphereGeometry(OD/2, 64, 32, 0, 2*PI, 0, PI/2)
  - Cone/transition: ConeGeometry or CylinderGeometry with different top/bottom radii
  - Nozzle: CylinderGeometry(nozzOD/2, nozzOD/2, nozzLen, 32) positioned on shell surface
  - Flange: CylinderGeometry(flangeOD/2, flangeOD/2, flangeThk, 64)
