CODE-SPECIFIC MODULE: Casting Radiography

REFERENCE RADIOGRAPHS:
  ASTM E446:  Steel castings up to 2" (51mm) thickness
  ASTM E186:  Steel castings 2" to 4.5" (51–114mm) thickness
  ASTM E280:  Steel castings 4.5" to 12" (114–305mm) thickness
  ASTM E2422: Digital reference images for castings
  ASTM E155:  Aluminum and magnesium castings

DISCONTINUITY TYPES (per ASTM category):
  Category A: Gas porosity (Types A1–A5 severity)
  Category B: Sand/slag inclusions (B1–B5)
  Category C: Shrinkage (C1–C5)
  Category D: Shrinkage + gas (D1–D5)
  Category E: Cracks (accept/reject binary — no severity grades)
  Category F: Inserts/chaplets
  Category G: Mottling

CRITICAL ZONES IN CASTINGS:
  - Section thickness transitions (hot spots — last to solidify, highest shrinkage risk)
  - Last-to-solidify regions at center mass (centerline shrinkage)
  - Gate and riser removal areas (stress concentration, potential tears)
  - Core support / chaplet locations (potential cold shuts, porosity clusters)
  - Sharp internal corners and re-entrant features (stress risers + shrinkage)
  - Weld repair areas (if any prior repairs noted on drawing)

SEVERITY MAPPING:
  CRITICAL: Section thickness change zones, areas near gates/risers, any cracks (Category E)
  HIGH:     High-mass regions (last to solidify), bore/hole intersections with casting walls
  MEDIUM:   Mid-section areas, general body casting — normal shrinkage probability
  LOW:      Thin uniform sections with well-controlled solidification

GEOMETRY NOTES:
  - Use freeform_solid primitive — provide bounding box dimensions
  - Identify internal passages as subtracted volumes (note in features as bore or core_print)
  - Section thickness map is critical for technique planning — note min/max thickness in dimensions
  - Complex castings may require multiple RT angles (report as separate inspection zones per face)
  - Use BoxGeometry or CylinderGeometry for simple casting shapes
  - Use LatheGeometry for rotationally symmetric castings (valve bodies, flanges, pump casings)

TECHNIQUE NOTES:
  - Source selection based on section thickness (T): T < 1" use X-ray 200-250kV; T 1-3" use Ir-192; T > 3" use Co-60
  - Multiple RT angles required for complex geometry — document all required exposures
  - DR/CR preferred for production castings — enables real-time process feedback
