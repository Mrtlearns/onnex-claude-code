## How Claude Uses This

When you run `/prime`, Claude reads this index and the individual TELOS files to understand:
- What you're ultimately working toward (MISSION)
- What you're actively pursuing right now (GOALS + PROJECTS)
- What's in the way and how you're addressing it (CHALLENGES + STRATEGIES)
- How you think and what you believe (BELIEFS)
- How you talk about what you do (NARRATIVES)
- Who you serve and what you know about them (CLIENTS)

This makes every Claude session context-aware from the first message.
